\*\*Problem\*\*: Buyers hate "Contact us for pricing"—it signals unpredictability and sales pressure.    
\*\*Our Approach\*\*: Transparent tiers (Basic, Premium, Enterprise) with clear deliverables and timelines.    
\*\*Impact\*\*: Faster buying decisions, higher inbound conversion, self-service qualification.

\#\#\# 2\. Database-Backed Everything (vs. Static Sites)  
\*\*Problem\*\*: Most agency sites are just HTML \+ forms that email to inbox.    
\*\*Our Approach\*\*: Every configurator, calculator, and interaction writes to a database; every lead is scored, tracked, nurtured.    
\*\*Impact\*\*: Clients get actionable pipeline data, not just traffic metrics.

\*\*Problem\*\*: Agencies give away whitepapers for email addresses, then spam generic newsletters.    
\*\*Our Approach\*\*: Interactive tools (configurators, calculators, quizzes) that deliver personalized outputs locked behind contact forms.    
\*\*Impact\*\*: Higher-intent leads, better segmentation, warmer handoff to sales.

\*\*Problem\*\*: GTMLAB and international agencies copy-paste Western UX patterns that don't resonate in SEA.    
\*\*Our Approach\*\*: Pricing in local currency, case studies from regional brands, compliance with local data laws, culturally adapted messaging.    
\*\*Impact\*\*: Faster trust-building, fewer localization issues, better conversion in target markets.

\*\*Problem\*\*: Agencies reinvent the wheel per project, leading to scope creep and inconsistent quality.
