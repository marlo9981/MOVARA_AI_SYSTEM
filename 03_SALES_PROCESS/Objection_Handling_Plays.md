MOVARA AI – OBJECTION HANDLING PLAYS

PURPOSE  
Capture common objections and provide simple, respectful ways to respond, while keeping the conversation focused on value.

FRAMEWORK  
Simple structure for each objection:  
1\. Acknowledge  
2\. Clarify  
3\. Reframe to value  
4\. Suggest a next step

COMMON OBJECTIONS

1\. “It’s too expensive.”  
\- Acknowledge: “I understand, it’s a meaningful investment.”  
\- Clarify: “Can you share what you were expecting or comparing this to?”  
\- Reframe: focus on revenue, saved time, and avoided mistakes.  
\- Next step: explore a lighter package or phased approach if needed.

2\. “We’re too busy / wrong timing.”  
\- Acknowledge: “That makes sense, timing is important.”  
\- Clarify: “What else is happening right now that makes this difficult?”  
\- Reframe: highlight the cost of delaying (lost leads, manual work).  
\- Next step: offer a realistic start date, or a smaller ‘Phase 0’ to prepare.

3\. “We’re not sure it will work for us.”  
\- Acknowledge: “That’s fair – you don’t want to gamble.”  
\- Clarify: “What specific risks or worries do you have?”  
\- Reframe: connect to similar use cases or patterns.  
\- Next step: propose a scoped pilot or clear success criteria.

4\. “We already have a website / agency.”  
\- Acknowledge: “Good that you’re already investing there.”  
\- Clarify: “What’s working well, and what isn’t?”  
\- Reframe: Movara AI focuses on conversion \+ automation, not just a redesign.  
\- Next step: offer an audit or focused improvement project.

NOTES  
\- Always respect the objection; do not push aggressively.  
\- Use objections to learn more about the real concern behind the words.  
\- Update this doc with real phrases prospects use over time.
