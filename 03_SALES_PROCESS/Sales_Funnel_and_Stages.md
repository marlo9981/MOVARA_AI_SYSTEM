MOVARA AI – SALES FUNNEL AND STAGES

PURPOSE  
Define the standard stages from initial interest to closed-won, with clear exit criteria for each stage.

STAGE 1 – INBOUND OR OUTBOUND LEAD  
Example sources:  
\- Website forms and interactive tools  
\- Referrals and introductions  
\- Outbound messages (LinkedIn, email, etc.)

Entry criteria:  
\- A lead submits a form, books a call, or responds positively to outreach.

Exit criteria:  
\- Lead is scheduled for a discovery call  
OR  
\- Lead is disqualified early (not ICP, no budget, wrong geography).

STAGE 2 – DISCOVERY CALL  
Goal:  
\- Understand context, pain, urgency, budget, and fit.

Key questions:  
\- What prompted you to explore this now?  
\- How are you currently generating and handling leads?  
\- What does success look like 6–12 months from now?  
\- Who else will be involved in the decision?

Exit criteria:  
\- Clear fit and rough budget confirmed  
\- Agreement on next step (e.g., deeper scoping, proposal, or demo)  
\- Or: marked as no fit / nurture later

STAGE 3 – SCOPING / DEMO (IF NEEDED)  
Goal:  
\- Show how Movara AI works, and scope the work at a high level.

Activities:  
\- Share relevant case examples or flows.  
\- Walk through how a typical Movara AI system would work for them.  
\- Clarify which package or custom scope matches their situation.

Exit criteria:  
\- Confirmed scope direction (package or custom)  
\- Agreement to receive a proposal with pricing and timeline

STAGE 4 – PROPOSAL AND DECISION  
Goal:  
\- Present a clear package, price, and timeline.  
\- Answer remaining questions and objections.

Activities:  
\- Present proposal (call \+ document).  
\- Walk through structure: problem, solution, scope, pricing, timeline.  
\- Handle objections (price, timing, priority, internal alignment).

Exit criteria:  
\- Verbal yes → move to contract \+ invoice  
\- Delayed decision → move to structured follow-up sequence  
\- Clear no → log reasons and close-lost

STAGE 5 – WIN, HANDOFF, AND KICKOFF  
Goal:  
\- Turn a yes into a clean start for delivery.

Activities:  
\- Send contract and invoice.  
\- Confirm start date and key stakeholders.  
\- Schedule kickoff meeting.  
\- Handoff to delivery playbook (04\_DELIVERY\_PLAYBOOKS).

Exit criteria:  
\- Contract signed and deposit received  
\- Internal handoff completed  
\- Project added to delivery pipeline

NOTES  
\- Keep the number of stages small and focused.  
\- Add real examples under each stage over time.
