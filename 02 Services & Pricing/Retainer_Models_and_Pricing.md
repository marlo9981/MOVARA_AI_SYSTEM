RETAINER MODELS AND PRICING

PURPOSE  
Define simple, clear retainer options for clients who want ongoing support after a project. Retainers should:  
\- Protect the relationship  
\- Provide predictable revenue  
\- Stay easy to explain and manage

GENERAL PRINCIPLES  
\- Retainers are offered after a successful project or to ICP-aligned clients.  
\- Each retainer clearly lists what is included and what is excluded.  
\- Retainers should be tied to outcomes (improvement and maintenance), not just “hours”.

EXAMPLE RETAINER MODELS

1\. LIGHTCARE RETAINER (SMALL)  
Goal:  
\- Keep the site and automations healthy and make small improvements.

Example inclusions:  
\- Monthly health check on website and automations.  
\- Small fixes and content updates (up to a set number per month).  
\- Basic reporting summary (traffic, leads, key issues).

Use when:  
\- Client is smaller but serious.  
\- They want a safety net and minor tweaks over time.

2\. GROWTHCARE RETAINER (CORE)  
Goal:  
\- Continually improve conversion and automation performance.

Example inclusions:  
\- Everything in LightCare.  
\- Monthly experiment or improvement (e.g. new landing page, A/B test, new automation branch).  
\- Deeper reporting and insight call (once a month).  
\- Reserved hours or scope for experimentation.

Use when:  
\- The client wants ongoing growth and optimisation.  
\- There are clear metrics to improve (conversion, lead quality, response time).

3\. SYSTEMCARE RETAINER (ADVANCED)  
Goal:  
\- Act as a long-term systems partner for complex setups.

Example inclusions:  
\- Everything in GrowthCare.  
\- Work across multiple properties, languages, or segments.  
\- Priority support for issues and high-impact changes.  
\- Quarterly strategic review and roadmap.

Use when:  
\- The client’s system is complex and business-critical.  
\- Multiple teams rely on the website and automations.

NOTES ON PRICING  
\- Each retainer should have:  
  \- A minimum term (for example 3 or 6 months).  
  \- A clear renewal or review point.  
\- Pricing should feel fair relative to:  
  \- The initial project cost.  
  \- The expected value of ongoing improvements.  
  \- The level of access and responsiveness promised.

HOW TO EXPLAIN RETAINERS TO CLIENTS  
\- “The project builds the initial system; the retainer keeps improving it and stops it from decaying.”  
\- “Without a retainer, things usually drift: content goes out of date, automations break, and opportunities are missed.”  
\- “With a retainer, you get a clear rhythm: review, improve, and grow over time.”
