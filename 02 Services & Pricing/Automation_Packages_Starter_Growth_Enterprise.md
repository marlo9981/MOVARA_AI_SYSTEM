AUTOMATION PACKAGES – STARTER / GROWTH / ENTERPRISE

PURPOSE OF THESE PACKAGES  
These automation packages define what a client gets beyond “just a website”. They are meant to be:  
\- Easy to understand  
\- Easy to compare  
\- Directly tied to revenue, saved time, and reduced manual work

DEFAULT ASSUMPTIONS  
\- Tools: CRM or pipeline tool (e.g. HubSpot / Pipedrive), email tool, form tools  
\- Market: SEA-based businesses fitting our ICP  
\- The website is either built by Movara AI or suitable for integration

OVERVIEW TABLE (HIGH LEVEL)

Starter  
\- Goal: get simple, reliable lead capture and basic follow-up in place.  
\- For: small teams taking first steps with automation.

Growth  
\- Goal: qualify leads better and reduce manual follow-up.  
\- For: teams with consistent lead flow who want smarter systems.

Enterprise  
\- Goal: orchestrate complex workflows, multiple teams, and advanced reporting.  
\- For: larger or more complex organisations with multiple products/markets.

────────────────────────────────  
STARTER AUTOMATION PACKAGE  
────────────────────────────────

Who it’s for  
\- Businesses who do not have any meaningful automation yet.  
\- Teams that rely on email inboxes or spreadsheets to manage leads.

Core outcomes  
\- Every website enquiry is captured in a structured way.  
\- Leads are acknowledged quickly.  
\- The team has a single, simple place to see and update leads.

Included components

1\. Lead Capture and Routing  
\- Standardised forms on key pages (contact, quote, demo, etc.)  
\- All form submissions pushed into a simple CRM or pipeline board.  
\- Basic lead fields: name, email, phone, company, interest, source.

2\. Instant Acknowledgement  
\- Auto-response email sent to new leads, confirming receipt and what to expect next.  
\- Optional WhatsApp or SMS notification to internal owner for high-value forms.

3\. Simple Pipeline View  
\- A basic pipeline with 3–4 stages (New → Contacted → Qualified → Closed).  
\- Ability to assign an owner for each lead.

4\. Basic Reporting  
\- Monthly summary of:  
  \- Number of leads  
  \- Basic breakdown by form or source  
  \- Simple conversion from New → Qualified

────────────────────────────────  
GROWTH AUTOMATION PACKAGE  
────────────────────────────────

Who it’s for  
\- Businesses with consistent lead volume.  
\- Teams that are losing time on manual follow-up and lead qualification.

Core outcomes  
\- Leads are automatically segmented by quality or interest.  
\- Follow-up sequences are standardised and automated.  
\- The sales team spends more time with warm, qualified leads.

Included components

1\. Advanced Lead Capture and Qualification  
\- Forms and tools that collect key qualification data (budget, timeline, use case).  
\- Lead scoring rules based on answers and behaviour (e.g. viewed pricing page).

2\. Nurture Sequences  
\- Email sequences for:  
  \- New leads who are not ready to talk yet.  
  \- Leads that downloaded a resource or used a calculator.  
\- Sequences tailored by segment (for example “Real Estate Developer” vs “Renovation Company”).

3\. Sales Follow-Up Automation  
\- Automated reminders for sales team to follow-up at specific intervals.  
\- Task creation in CRM when certain conditions are met (e.g. high-scoring lead with no contact).

4\. Calendaring and Booking Integration  
\- Integration with booking tool for demos or consultations.  
\- Automatic confirmation and reminder emails for meetings.

5\. Reporting and Insights  
\- Monthly or quarterly view of:  
  \- Leads by segment  
  \- Lead scores and close rates  
  \- Performance of nurture sequences (opens, clicks, replies)

────────────────────────────────  
ENTERPRISE AUTOMATION PACKAGE  
────────────────────────────────

Who it’s for  
\- Businesses with multiple teams, products, or markets.  
\- Organisations that need tighter integration between marketing, sales, and operations.

Core outcomes  
\- Complex journeys across multiple touchpoints are orchestrated.  
\- Data is synchronised between tools.  
\- Leadership has better visibility into performance.

Included components

1\. Multi-Channel Journeys  
\- Coordinated automations across:  
  \- Email  
  \- SMS / WhatsApp (where appropriate)  
  \- In-app or on-site messaging (if available)  
\- Branching logic based on behaviour (clicked, attended, replied, etc.)

2\. Advanced Segmentation and Scoring  
\- Detailed segmentation based on:  
  \- Behaviour (pages visited, tools used)  
  \- Profile (industry, size, region)  
  \- Engagement (opens, clicks, replies)  
\- Scoring that integrates with sales prioritisation.

3\. CRM and Tool Integrations  
\- Deeper integrations between CRM, marketing automation, and ops tools.  
\- Clear source-of-truth rules (which system is master for which data).

4\. Operational Automations  
\- Automations that support delivery or operations (e.g. onboarding flows, internal notifications, task routing).  
\- Handovers between teams (marketing → sales, sales → delivery).

5\. Custom Dashboards  
\- Dashboards that combine:  
  \- Lead and funnel metrics  
  \- Campaign performance  
  \- Sales and revenue outcomes  
\- Tailored to leadership or team needs.
