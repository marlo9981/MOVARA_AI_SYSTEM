## Purpose

Define the visual requirements for case study pages on the Atrellis website. Case studies are the deepest form of proof — they tell the full story of a project from problem to solution to result.

---

## Case Study Visual Structure

Every case study page follows this visual narrative:

### 1. Hero Image
- **Type:** Best after shot or before/after split
- **Purpose:** Immediate impact and context
- **Specs:** Full-width, high resolution, dramatic composition
- **Alt text:** Project type + location + key result

### 2. The Problem (Before Section)
- **Images:** 2-4 before photos
- **Layout:** Grid or carousel
- **Purpose:** Establish the starting condition and pain points
- **Captions:** Describe what the viewer is seeing and why it matters

### 3. The Process (During Section)
- **Images:** 3-5 process shots
- **Layout:** Timeline or step-by-step layout
- **Purpose:** Show professionalism, expertise, and what happens behind the scenes
- **Captions:** Explain what's happening and why it matters

### 4. The Solution (After Section)
- **Images:** 4-6 after photos
- **Layout:** Mix of wide, medium, and detail
- **Purpose:** Showcase the transformation and quality
- **Captions:** Highlight specific features and benefits

### 5. The Result (Lifestyle/Testimonial Section)
- **Images:** 1-2 lifestyle shots
- **Purpose:** Show the space being enjoyed
- **Optional:** Client photo with testimonial quote

---

## Minimum Image Count Per Case Study

| Section | Minimum Images |
|---|---|
| Hero | 1 |
| Before | 2 |
| Process | 3 |
| After | 4 |
| Lifestyle | 1 |
| **Total minimum** | **11** |

---

## Image Specifications for Case Studies

### Hero Image
- **Size:** 1920px minimum width
- **Format:** WebP (primary), JPEG fallback
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 or 21:9
- **File size:** < 400KB optimized

### Body Images
- **Size:** 1200px minimum width
- **Format:** WebP (primary), JPEG fallback
- **Aspect ratio:** 4:3 or 3:2
- **File size:** < 250KB optimized

### Detail/Thumbnail Images
- **Size:** 600px minimum width
- **Format:** WebP (primary), JPEG fallback
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 or 4:3
- **File size:** < 100KB optimized

---

## Before/After Slider Requirements

For case studies featuring before/after sliders:

- **Pair matching:** Same angle, same framing, same lens
- **Alignment:** Pixel-perfect overlay possible
- **Lighting:** Similar exposure (adjust in post if needed)
- **Minimum pairs:** 1 per case study, 3+ ideal
- **Interaction:** Slider or click-to-reveal on site

---

## Layout Patterns

### Pattern A: Full Story (Premium Case Studies)
- Hero → Problem → Process → Solution → Result
- 11+ images
- Used for flagship projects and key service categories

### Pattern B: Quick Case Study
- Hero → Before/After → Key Details
- 6-8 images
- Used for secondary projects or simpler scopes

### Pattern C: Gallery Entry
- Hero → 3-4 best shots
- 4-5 images
- Used for projects in the main gallery without full case study treatment

---

## Notes

- Not every project needs a full case study — reserve Pattern A for the strongest examples
- Before/after sliders are the highest-converting visual element
- Client testimonials with photos increase trust significantly
- Consider video walkthroughs for premium case studies in Phase 2
