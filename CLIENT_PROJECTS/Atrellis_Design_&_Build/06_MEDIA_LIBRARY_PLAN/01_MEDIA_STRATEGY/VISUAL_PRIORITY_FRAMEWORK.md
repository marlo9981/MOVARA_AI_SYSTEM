## Purpose

Define how to prioritize visual assets so time and budget go toward the media that matters most for conversion and brand building.

---

## Priority Tiers

### Tier 1 — Conversion Critical (Must Have Before Launch)

These visuals directly impact whether a visitor becomes a lead:

- Homepage hero image
- Before/after project photos (minimum 3 projects)
- Project gallery images (minimum 10 projects)
- Service page hero images (one per service category)
- Trust/proof section visuals

**Rule:** Launch cannot happen without Tier 1 visuals.

### Tier 2 — Trust Building (Should Have Before Launch)

These visuals strengthen credibility and reduce buyer anxiety:

- Team photos (leadership, key staff)
- Process/behind-the-scenes shots
- Detail shots of craftsmanship
- Office/workspace photos
- Testimonial photos (with client permission)

**Rule:** Aim to have these at launch; acceptable to fill in within 30 days post-launch.

### Tier 3 — Enhancement (Post-Launch)

These visuals improve experience but are not launch-critical:

- Video walkthroughs of projects
- 3D renders or diagrams
- Lifestyle photography
- Seasonal or promotional visuals
- Additional project coverage

**Rule:** Build these over time as projects complete.

---

## Investment Guide

| Tier | Budget Allocation | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 60% of media budget | Before launch |
| Tier 2 | 30% of media budget | Before launch or within 30 days |
| Tier 3 | 10% of media budget | Ongoing |

---

## Decision Framework

When deciding whether to invest in a visual:

1. **Does it prove capability?** — Project photos > everything else
2. **Does it reduce anxiety?** — Before/afters, team photos, process shots
3. **Does it differentiate?** — Unique angles, exclusive content
4. **Does it explain?** — Diagrams, renders for complex concepts
5. **Does it decorate?** — Lowest priority; only if budget allows

---

## Replacement Priority

When placeholder images exist:

1. Replace Tier 1 placeholders first
2. Replace Tier 2 placeholders second
3. Replace Tier 3 placeholders as budget allows
