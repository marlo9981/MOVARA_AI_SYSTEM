## Purpose

This is the canonical source of truth for all visual media strategy for the Atrellis website. It defines why media matters, what types of visuals we need, and how they support business objectives.

---

## Media Philosophy

Atrellis sells trust and transformation. Visual media is the primary vehicle for both:

- **Trust** — real photos of real work prove capability
- **Transformation** — before/after imagery shows the value delivered

Stock photography is forbidden. Every visual must be authentic to Atrellis's actual work, team, or brand.

---

## Media Categories

| Category | Purpose | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Project Media | Proof of work and quality | P0 — Critical |
| Hero Visuals | First impression and emotional hook | P0 — Critical |
| Team/People | Humanize the brand, build trust | P1 — Important |
| Process/Behind-the-Scenes | Show professionalism and expertise | P1 — Important |
| Product/Material | Showcase specific offerings and brands | P2 — Supporting |
| Illustration/Diagram | Explain complex concepts | P2 — Supporting |
| Office/Workspace | Establish legitimacy | P3 — Nice to have |

---

## Visual Principles

1. **Authentic over polished** — real work beats stock every time
2. **Before/after is king** — the single most powerful conversion tool
3. **Detail proves quality** — close-ups of craftsmanship build confidence
4. **People matter** — show the team behind the work
5. **Consistency** — cohesive visual style across all pages
6. **Performance** — optimized for web without sacrificing quality

## Media Sources

| Source | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| On-site photography | Primary | Project shoots, team photos |
| Video captures | Secondary | Stills from video walkthroughs |
| 3D renders | Supporting | For configurators and future platform |
| Product photography | Supporting | Supplier-provided (with permission) |

---

## Quality Standards

- No stock photography
- Minimum resolution: 3000px longest edge for raw captures
- Consistent color grading across all project photos
- Natural lighting preferred
- Straight lines, clean compositions
- Real Atrellis work only
