# Content Production Pipeline

## Workflow Steps
1. **Strategy/Brief**: Define the goal, category, and target pillar.
2. **Outline**: Create a structural map using standard blocks (Hero, Scope, Process, etc.).
3. **Drafting**: Create the content (Human or AI-assisted).
4. **Editorial Review**: Ensure tone and brand alignment.
5. **SEO & CTA Audit**: Pass the On-Page Checklist.
6. **Publishing**: CMS entry with proper metadata and schema.
7. **Retro**: Review performance after 90 days.

## Definition of Done
- Content is 100% aligned with a Service Category.
- All structural blocks are correctly used.
- Internal links to Hubs/Spokes are present.
- At least one primary CTA is functioning.
