# Measurement and Iteration

## Core Metrics
- **Category Traffic**: Which service categories are attracting the most interest?
- **Engagement**: Time on page and scroll depth on Educational Guides.
- **Conversion**: Leads generated from specific content clusters.
- **Assisted Conversion**: Content that is viewed before a user launches a platform tool.

## The Feedback Loop
- **Quarterly Audit**: Review the top and bottom 10% of content.
- **Signal Triggers**: If a cluster is high-traffic but low-conversion, revisit the CTA and internal linking rules.
- **Iteration**: Update pillars and templates based on what real users are clicking and searching.
