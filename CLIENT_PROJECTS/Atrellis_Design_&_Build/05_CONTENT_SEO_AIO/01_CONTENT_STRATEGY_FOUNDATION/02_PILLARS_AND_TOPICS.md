# Pillars and Topics

## Content Pillars
Atrellis content is organized into five long-term pillars. These ensure topical authority and support our transition into a platform-led brand.

### 1. Strategic Planning & Feasibility
- **Focus**: Zoning, permits, site assessment, and architectural constraints.
- **Category Match**: Architecture & Design, Pre-Construction.
- **CTAs**: Book a Consultation, Download Planning Guide.

### 2. The Design-Build Methodology
- **Focus**: The benefits of integrated delivery, project management, and cost-control through unified workflows.
- **Category Match**: All Master Categories.
- **CTAs**: Start Your Quote, Watch Process Video.

### 3. Material & Product Systems
- **Focus**: High-end finishes, sustainable systems, and smart home integration.
- **Category Match**: Interior Design, Kitchen/Bath Specialties.
- **CTAs**: Browse Material Catalog, Enter Configurator.

### 4. Visualisation & Reality
- **Focus**: From floorplans to 3D renders; how we use technology to eliminate guesswork.
- **Category Match**: Architecture & Design, Visualisation Services.
- **CTAs**: View Project Gallery, Request Render.

### 5. Investment & Value (Aftercare)
- **Focus**: ROI of renovations, maintenance, and long-term property value.
- **Category Match**: Real Estate Value, Project Management.
- **CTAs**: Lead to Dashboard, Subscription Services.

## Topic Cluster Shapes (Generic Patterns)
- **The Pillar Hub**: A comprehensive guide to a major category (e.g., "The Complete Guide to Kitchen Design-Build").
- **The Solution Spoke**: Addressing a specific pain point (e.g., "How to Manage Renovation Budgets in Heritage Zones").
- **The Tool Spoke**: Supporting a digital feature (e.g., "How to Use the Atrellis Floorplan Uploader").
