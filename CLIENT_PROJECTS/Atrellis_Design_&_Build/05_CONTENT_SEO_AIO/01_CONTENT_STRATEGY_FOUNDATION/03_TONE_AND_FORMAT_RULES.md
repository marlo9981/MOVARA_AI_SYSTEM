# Tone and Format Rules

## Atrellis Voice
The voice of Atrellis is **Premium, Calm, and Competent**. We are the experts who have seen it all and have a system for everything. We are digitally native but never robotic.

## Tone by Context
- **Service Pages**: Decisive, authoritative, and CTA-focused. We state what we do and why it works.
- **Educational Guides**: Patient, explanatory, and helpful. We are mentors in the design-build process.
- **Tool UX Copy**: Short, directive, and reassuring. We guide the user through the digital experience without friction.

## Formatting Standards
- **Headings**: Use H1 for titles, H2 for major sections, and H3 for sub-points. Avoid H4 unless absolutely necessary.
- **Paragraphs**: Maximum 3-4 sentences. Use whitespace to ensure scannability.
- **Bullets**: Use for lists of features, benefits, or process steps. Ensure parallel structure in bullet points.
- **FAQs**: Use a consistent Q&A format that addresses real user anxieties.

## Visual Integration
- **Contextual Renders**: Use renders to illustrate concepts that haven't been built yet.
- **Before/After Labels**: Always clearly label project transitions.
- **Captions**: Every image must have a caption that reinforces the category topic (e.g., "Modernist kitchen island design featuring integrated smart lighting").
- **SEO Alignment**: Visuals must support the primary intent of the page, using descriptive alt-text and file names.
