# Content Strategy Foundation

## Objective
The Atrellis content strategy is designed to position the brand as a premium, digitally-capable design-build partner. Content acts as the "connective tissue" between high-level brand positioning (`01_BRAND_FOUNDATION`) and functional service architecture (`04_SERVICE_CATEGORY_SYSTEM`).

## The Role of Content
Content at Atrellis serves three primary functions:
1. **Support Service Architecture**: Every piece of content must reinforce a specific Service Category or Template.
2. **Pre-Qualification**: Content should answer complex questions regarding scope, budget, and process, reducing friction in the lead-capture and quoting flows.
3. **Platform Onboarding**: Strategic content prepares users for future tools, such as configurators and floorplan-to-render workflows, by building trust in our digital capabilities.

## Core Principles
- **Clarity Over Cleverness**: We prioritize understanding over industry jargon or flowery prose.
- **Usefulness Over Volume**: We do not publish for the sake of publishing. Every piece must solve a problem or advance a journey.
- **Search-Aware, Not Keyword-Driven**: We design for user intent first; search optimization follows structure.
- **Always Anchored**: Every asset must link back to a primary Service Category and a clear CTA.

## Content Types and Mapping
- **Service Pages**: High-intent landing pages built on standardized templates.
- **Educational Guides**: Deep-dives into planning, materials, and "Design-Build" methodologies.
- **Visual Galleries**: Proof-of-concept renders and project photography tied to specific categories.
- **Process Checklists**: Step-by-step resources that mirror our internal project phases.
- **Tool Resources**: Support content for configurators, budget calculators, and dashboard features.
