# Content Library Model

## The Role of the Library
The Atrellis Content Library is not a "blog" silo; it is a repository of knowledge that feeds our service pages and tools.

### Content Types
- **In-Depth Articles**: Explaining "The Design-Build Advantage" or "How Technology is Changing Renovations."
- **How-To Guides**: Practical advice for homeowners (e.g., "How to Prepare for a 6-Month Build").
- **Resource Checklists**: Downloadable PDF or web-based checklists for planning phases.
- **Tool Explainers**: Content that teaches users how to get the most out of our digital tools.

## Strategic Goal
- **80% Evergreen**: Content that remains relevant for years.
- **20% Timely**: Updates on industry trends, new technology, or local regulation changes.
- **Conversion focus**: Every article must lead to a related Service Category or a platform tool.
