# Social Proof and Portfolio SEO

## Case Study Structure
Project pages are vital trust signals and SEO assets.
- **Project Name**: Includes the service and general location.
- **The Brief**: Describes the client's problem.
- **The Solution**: Highlights the Atrellis methodology and tools used.
- **The Result**: High-quality visuals and client quotes.

## Testimonial Architecture
- **Verification**: Link to GMB or Trustpilot where possible.
- **Topic Matching**: Tag testimonials by Service Category (e.g., "Kitchen," "Extension") so they can be pulled into relevant service pages.
- **Markup**: Use `Review` schema for every major testimonial.
