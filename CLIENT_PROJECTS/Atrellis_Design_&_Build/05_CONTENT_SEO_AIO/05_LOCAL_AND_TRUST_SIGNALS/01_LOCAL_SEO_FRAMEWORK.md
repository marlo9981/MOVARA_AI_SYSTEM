# Local SEO Framework

## Principled Local Search
Atrellis competes on quality and capability, not on being "the closest" contractor. Our local strategy reflects this.

### Geographic Signals
- **Service Areas**: Listed clearly on the Contact and About pages.
- **Contextual Local Content**: Mentioning local planning authorities or suppliers in Case Studies.
- **GMB (Google My Business)**: Our primary local anchor. Must be kept updated with high-quality photos and reviews.

### Anti-Spam Rules
- No "City + Service" footer clouds.
- No auto-generated "Service in [City Name]" pages.
- Local pages must provide genuine value (e.g., "Renovating in Chelsea: A Guide to Local Permits").
