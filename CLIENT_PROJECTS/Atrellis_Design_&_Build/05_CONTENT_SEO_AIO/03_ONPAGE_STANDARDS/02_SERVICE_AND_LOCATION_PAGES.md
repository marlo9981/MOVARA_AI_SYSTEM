# Service and Location Pages

## Service Page Pattern
Service pages are the transactional heart of the site. They follow the structures defined in `04_SERVICE_CATEGORY_SYSTEM`.

### Required Sections
1. **Hero**: Clear statement of service + category.
2. **Value Prop**: Why Atrellis is the best choice for this specific service.
3. **Scope of Work**: Precise details of what is included.
4. **The Process**: How our platform-led workflow applies here.
5. **Social Proof**: Reviews or projects specific to this service.

## Location Page Policy
Atrellis avoids "Doorway Pages." We only create location-specific pages if we have:
- A physical presence or a significant portfolio in that area.
- Unique local requirements (e.g., "London Heritage Zone Permitting").

### Local Signal Rules
- Location signals live in the **footer**, **Contact page**, and **Case Studies**.
- Never sacrifice page quality for the sake of inserting a city name.
- Localized service pages must be at least 70% unique from their generic counterparts.
