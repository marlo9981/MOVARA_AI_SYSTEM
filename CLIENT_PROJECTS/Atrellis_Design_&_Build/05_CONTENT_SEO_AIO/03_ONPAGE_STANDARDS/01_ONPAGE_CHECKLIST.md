# On-Page Checklist

Every indexable page on Atrellis must pass this checklist before publication:

## Metadata & Titles
- [ ] **Title Tag**: Under 60 characters; includes primary topic and brand name.
- [ ] **Meta Description**: Under 155 characters; includes a clear value prop and CTA.
- [ ] **URL Slug**: Clean, short, and includes the primary keyword.

## Structure & Content
- [ ] **H1 Heading**: Only one per page; clearly states the page's purpose.
- [ ] **Hierarchy**: H2s and H3s are used logically to break up content.
- [ ] **Intro**: First 100 words establish the Category context and user intent.
- [ ] **Blocks**: Includes at least one Scope, Process, and Proof block.
- [ ] **Internal Links**: Links to at least one parent Hub and one related Spoke.

## Quality & Conversion
- [ ] **Tone**: Meets the "Premium, Calm, Competent" standard.
- [ ] **CTA**: At least one primary and one secondary CTA are visible.
- [ ] **Visuals**: All images have descriptive alt-text and high-quality captions.
- [ ] **Readability**: Avoids complex jargon; passes a standard scannability test.
