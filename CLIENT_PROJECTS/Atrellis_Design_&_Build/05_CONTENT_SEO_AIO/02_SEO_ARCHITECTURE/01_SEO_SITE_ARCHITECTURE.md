# SEO Site Architecture

## High-Level Hierarchy
The Atrellis site follows a logical flow that prioritizes service depth and platform utility:

1. **Homepage**: Brand authority and portal to major hubs.
2. **Category Hubs**: Indexable pages for Master Service Categories (e.g., /kitchens, /whole-home).
3. **Service Pages**: Specific service offerings (e.g., /kitchens/bespoke-cabinetry).
4. **Content Library**: Educational articles, guides, and project case studies.
5. **Tools & Flows**: Non-indexed or specifically gated tools (Configurators, Dashboards).

## Visibility Rules
- **Indexed Levels**: Homepage, Category Hubs, Service Pages, and Library Hubs.
- **Selective Indexing**: Project pages (Case Studies) are indexed only if they have significant unique content.
- **No-Index**: Search result pages, temporary landing pages, and user-specific dashboard views.

## IA Integrity
- **No Thin Pages**: We do not create pages for single keywords.
- **No Doorways**: Avoid creating multiple pages for the same service in different neighborhoods unless there is unique local content.
- **Navigational Clarity**: SEO requirements must never compromise the simplicity of the main navigation menu.
