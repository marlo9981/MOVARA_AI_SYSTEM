# Internal Linking Rules

## The Linking Framework
Internal links at Atrellis serve as "conversion rails" that guide users deeper into the platform.

### Patterns
- **Hub to Spoke**: Category Hubs link to all related Service Pages and Guides.
- **Spoke to Hub**: Service Pages always link back to their parent Category Hub.
- **Contextual In-Text**: Blog articles link to relevant Service Pages when a specific capability is mentioned.
- **Cross-Service**: Only link between services if they are naturally complementary (e.g., Architecture linking to Interior Design).

### Constraints
- **Maximum Links**: No more than 3-5 contextual links per 500 words of prose.
- **Button Priority**: Primary CTAs must be distinct from standard text links.
- **No Circular Links**: Avoid linking to the current page or redundant self-referential loops.

## Links as Data
- Internal links should be tracked via UTMs or custom attributes to understand how users transition from educational content to transactional tools.
