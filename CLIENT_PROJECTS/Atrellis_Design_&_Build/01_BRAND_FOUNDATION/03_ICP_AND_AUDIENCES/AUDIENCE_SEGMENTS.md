# Audience Segments

## Segment 1: Homeowners with practical upgrade intent

These are people who already know they need something done, but want a provider they can trust.

Typical entry interests:
- blinds
- roofing
- waterproofing
- balcony works
- renovation improvements

What matters most:
- clarity
- reliability
- visible professionalism
- easy next steps

## Segment 2: Homeowners with broader transformation intent

These people are not only solving one isolated issue.
They may be thinking about broader upgrades, coordinated improvements, or a more complete redesign/build scope.

What matters most:
- one-stop capability
- premium confidence
- less coordination burden
- stronger project structure

## Segment 3: Category-led searchers

These visitors arrive through specific searches and may not know Atrellis yet as a broader brand.

Examples:
- zipblinds-related intent
- roofing system intent
- balcony specialist intent
- blinds comparison intent
- waterproofing problem intent

What matters most:
- immediate relevance
- category depth
- proof and authority
- trust fast

## Segment 4: Comparison-stage buyers

These visitors are already comparing providers.

They are asking:
- who seems more serious
- who seems more premium
- who seems easier to work with
- who looks more trustworthy
- who reduces my risk

What matters most:
- trust signals
- differentiation
- clean presentation
- confidence in next steps

## Segment 5: Digitally expectation-heavy buyers

These people judge the business partly through the digital experience itself.

They are more sensitive to:
- outdated layouts
- weak structure
- unclear navigation
- low-effort presentation
- missing information

What matters most:
- polished experience
- guided flow
- cleaner information hierarchy
- smoother inquiry experience

## Segmentation rule

Atrellis should not build a separate identity for every small audience.

Instead, it should:
- identify the most commercially meaningful segments,
- structure the website to serve them,
- and let content depth support narrower intent where needed.
