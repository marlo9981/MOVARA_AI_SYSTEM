# 03_ICP_AND_AUDIENCES

## Purpose

This folder defines who Atrellis is for.

Its role is to identify:
- the ideal customer profiles,
- the most important audience segments,
- what each audience cares about,
- what problems they are trying to solve,
- what makes them trust or hesitate,
- and how Atrellis should speak to them.

This folder should prevent the brand from becoming too broad, too vague, or too generic.

## What belongs here

- Ideal customer profile definitions
- Key audience segments
- Buyer motivations
- Pain points and anxieties
- Trust triggers
- Audience-specific expectations
- Differences between customer types

## What does not belong here

- Full page copy
- Keyword lists
- Technical implementation details
- Competitor teardown notes
- Raw service descriptions without audience interpretation

Those belong elsewhere.

## Core files in this folder

- `ICP_MASTER.md`
- `AUDIENCE_SEGMENTS.md`
- `BUYER_NEEDS_AND_MOTIVATIONS.md`
- `NOTES.md`

## Source of truth

The main source of truth is:

- `ICP_MASTER.md`

## Usage rules

- Keep segments useful, not overly fictional.
- Do not create fake personas with unnecessary details.
- Focus on buying context, trust factors, needs, and behavior.
- Define audiences in a way that actually helps structure the site and messaging.
