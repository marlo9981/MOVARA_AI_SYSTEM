# ICP Master

## Purpose

This file defines the most important ideal customer profiles for Atrellis.

The goal is not to describe every possible customer.
The goal is to identify the audiences most worth attracting and converting.

## Core ICP definition

The strongest Atrellis customer is someone who values:
- trust,
- premium presentation,
- organized execution,
- confidence in decision-making,
- and a smoother path from evaluation to action.

They are not only buying a service.
They are buying confidence, convenience, and clarity.

## Primary ICP 01
### Name
Homeowner seeking premium renovation-related solutions

### Description
A homeowner looking for renovation, balcony, roofing, blinds, waterproofing, or related upgrade solutions who wants a provider that feels more trustworthy and more organized than the usual market options.

### What they want
- confidence in the company
- clean and clear explanations
- premium presentation
- reassurance that the provider can actually deliver
- smoother inquiry experience
- less confusion and less risk

### What they fear
- bad workmanship
- poor communication
- hidden problems
- unclear pricing
- providers that feel messy or unreliable
- making the wrong choice

### Why Atrellis fits
Atrellis is positioned to reduce decision friction and increase trust through stronger presentation, structure, and future-ready guidance.

## Primary ICP 02
### Name
Home or property owner with more complex scope

### Description
A customer with broader or more layered needs, such as full-home renovation, combined indoor and outdoor work, or multi-system requirements.

### What they want
- one capable partner
- less need to coordinate multiple vendors
- confidence that the provider can manage interconnected work
- stronger oversight and better organization

### What they fear
- fragmented coordination
- inconsistent quality across vendors
- project confusion
- wasted time due to poor planning

### Why Atrellis fits
The one-stop design-and-build positioning and structured digital presentation support this audience well.

## Secondary ICP 03
### Name
Customer evaluating a specific product-system category

### Description
A customer entering through one category such as zipblinds, roofing systems, blinds, balcony works, or waterproofing, but who may later expand into broader needs.

### What they want
- category clarity
- fast understanding
- proof of capability
- product/service distinctions
- confidence in a specific solution

### What they fear
- generic category content
- unclear differences between options
- lack of proof
- wasting time on weak providers

### Why Atrellis fits
Atrellis can win this audience through strong category pages and then expand trust into broader services.

## Secondary ICP 04
### Name
Digitally influenced buyer who values efficiency

### Description
A buyer who expects a cleaner, more modern, more guided digital experience and is more likely to respond to structured content, helpful flows, and better UX.

### What they want
- fast clarity
- clean browsing
- confidence-building structure
- digital convenience
- easy inquiry steps

### What they fear
- outdated websites
- unclear offers
- poor digital experience
- friction in getting started

### Why Atrellis fits
The project is intentionally designed to feel more digitally mature than the category norm.

## ICP rule

The best Atrellis customers are not necessarily the cheapest or fastest buyers.

They are the people most likely to value:
- premium trust,
- better structure,
- lower-friction progress,
- and a more complete solution.

That is the audience the brand should optimize for.
