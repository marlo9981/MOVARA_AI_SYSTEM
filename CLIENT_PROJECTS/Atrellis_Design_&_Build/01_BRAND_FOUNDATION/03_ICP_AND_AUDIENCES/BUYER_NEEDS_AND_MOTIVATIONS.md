# Buyer Needs and Motivations

## Core buyer needs

Across segments, the main needs are:

- trust
- clarity
- lower perceived risk
- proof of capability
- simpler decision-making
- clear next steps
- confidence that the provider can actually deliver

## Core buyer motivations

At a deeper level, people are motivated by:

- wanting their property to improve
- wanting a result that looks and feels good
- wanting fewer headaches
- wanting less uncertainty
- wanting a provider that seems dependable
- wanting to avoid regret

## What increases confidence

Confidence tends to increase when Atrellis provides:

- better structure
- cleaner categorization
- premium visual presentation
- specific explanations
- visible process logic
- trust and authority signals
- lower-friction inquiry steps

## What creates hesitation

Hesitation tends to increase when the experience feels:

- too generic
- too cluttered
- unclear
- too salesy
- too broad without structure
- weak on proof
- difficult to understand quickly

## Message implication

Messaging should not only describe what Atrellis does.

It should also reduce the emotional and practical friction of moving forward.

## Website implication

Every important page should answer:
- what is this,
- why should I trust it,
- does this fit my need,
- what should I do next,
- and what happens after that
