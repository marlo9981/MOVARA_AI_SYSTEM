# Brand Voice Guidelines

## Core voice

The Atrellis voice should feel:

- premium
- clear
- calm
- structured
- confident
- modern
- trustworthy
- helpful

## Core voice rule

Atrellis should sound like a capable brand that knows what it is doing and makes things easier to understand.

The voice should not sound like:
- loud advertising
- generic agency hype
- generic contractor cliché
- overdesigned luxury fluff
- startup-tech jargon without grounding

## Tone priorities

In order of importance, the voice should optimize for:

1. clarity
2. trust
3. structure
4. confidence
5. premium feel
6. persuasion

## Writing principles

### Principle 1: Clear before clever
If a line sounds stylish but becomes less clear, rewrite it.

### Principle 2: Calm confidence
The voice should feel controlled, not needy.

### Principle 3: Specific beats generic
Prefer exact explanations over vague claims.

### Principle 4: Structure is part of tone
Use clean phrasing and logical grouping.
The writing itself should feel organized.

### Principle 5: Persuasion through confidence, not pressure
The brand should guide people forward without sounding pushy.

## Voice personality

If turned into human traits, the Atrellis voice should feel like:
- composed
- sharp
- dependable
- design-aware
- commercially smart
- quietly premium

## Voice warning

Do not write Atrellis like:
- "we are your trusted partner in excellence"
- "your one-stop solution for all your needs"
- "innovative cutting-edge synergy"
- "transforming spaces with passion and dedication"

These sound generic and reduce perceived quality.
