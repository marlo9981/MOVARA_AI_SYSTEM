# Tone Examples

## Homepage hero direction

### Better
A more organized, more confident way to explore renovation-related solutions.

### Why it works
It sounds premium, clear, and different without exaggerating.

### Worse
Your one-stop trusted renovation partner for all your dream needs.

### Why it fails
It sounds generic, cluttered, and low-trust.

---

## Service explanation direction

### Better
Designed to help you evaluate the right solution more clearly and move forward with more confidence.

### Why it works
It supports trust and decision-making.

### Worse
We deliver top-quality premium solutions tailored to your unique requirements.

### Why it fails
It is vague and interchangeable with almost any business.

---

## Process wording direction

### Better
A cleaner path from discovery to decision to next steps.

### Why it works
It is simple, premium, and process-oriented.

### Worse
Our seamless innovative workflow transforms your customer journey.

### Why it fails
It sounds like generic marketing jargon.

---

## Brand description direction

### Better
Atrellis is a premium design-and-build and product-systems brand built around clarity, trust, and a more structured customer experience.

### Why it works
It sounds grounded and strategic.

### Worse
Atrellis is a revolutionary next-generation ecosystem redefining the way spaces are transformed.

### Why it fails
It sounds inflated and weakens trust.

---

## Voice calibration rule

If a sentence could appear on almost any generic contractor or agency website, it is probably not good enough for Atrellis.
