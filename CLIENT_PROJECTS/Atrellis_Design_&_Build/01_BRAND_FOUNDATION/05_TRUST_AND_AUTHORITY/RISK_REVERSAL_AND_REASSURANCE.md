# Risk Reversal and Reassurance

## Purpose

This file defines how Atrellis should reduce perceived risk.

Customers often hesitate not because they lack interest, but because they fear making the wrong choice.

The brand should reduce that fear.

## Core risks customers feel

Common risks include:
- choosing the wrong provider
- unclear scope
- poor communication
- low-quality delivery
- disorganized coordination
- wasting time
- moving forward without enough confidence

## Reassurance strategy

Atrellis should reduce perceived risk through:

- clearer structure
- better explanations
- more visible professionalism
- better next-step logic
- stronger process framing
- more confidence-building page flow
- controlled and believable claims

## What reassurance should sound like

Reassurance should feel:
- calm
- clear
- grounded
- professional
- helpful
- confidence-building

It should not feel:
- desperate
- defensive
- overly salesy
- exaggerated
- pushy

## Reassurance moments

Important places to reduce risk:

- early homepage trust layer
- service-page explanation areas
- comparison and category clarification areas
- inquiry forms
- post-CTA transition points
- FAQ and support content

## Message rule

Good reassurance does not mean saying "don't worry" repeatedly.

It means making the experience itself feel more trustworthy and easier to act on.

## Strategic rule

Every important friction point should be improved by one of these:
- clearer explanation
- better proof
- cleaner process visibility
- stronger trust signal
- better CTA framing
