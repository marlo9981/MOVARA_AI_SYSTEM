# Proof Points Library

## Purpose

This file defines the types of proof that should support the Atrellis brand.

The goal is not to force all proof into every page.
The goal is to know what kinds of evidence exist and where they are most useful.

## Proof categories

### Category 1: Capability proof
Signals that Atrellis can actually deliver.

Examples:
- project examples
- before and after references
- service-specific knowledge
- system-specific explanations
- visible range handled well

### Category 2: Process proof
Signals that there is a real and reliable way of working.

Examples:
- clear process explanation
- inquiry and follow-up structure
- design-to-build pathway
- organized next steps
- visible operating logic

### Category 3: Brand association proof
Signals that Atrellis is connected to credible suppliers, sponsors, product systems, or recognized brands where relevant.

Examples:
- supplier relationships
- sponsor references
- material and system brand mentions
- recognized product associations

### Category 4: Scope proof
Signals that Atrellis can handle more than one isolated need in a coherent way.

Examples:
- combined service coverage
- one-stop positioning
- coordinated scope examples
- category family structure

### Category 5: Knowledge proof
Signals that Atrellis understands the category in a deeper way.

Examples:
- educational content
- comparison content
- FAQs
- pain-point explanations
- useful buying guidance

### Category 6: Reassurance proof
Signals that the customer is less likely to regret moving forward.

Examples:
- transparent explanation
- reduced ambiguity
- clear next-step expectations
- realistic positioning
- visible accountability

## Proof usage rule

The stronger the claim, the stronger the proof required.

If Atrellis claims to be:
- premium,
- organized,
- expert,
- or a better way to move forward,

then the website should visibly support that through proof and structure.

## Placement rule

Different proof should appear in different places:

- Home = broad trust and confidence proof
- Service pages = relevance and capability proof
- Category pages = depth and authority proof
- About pages = credibility and company proof
- Inquiry areas = reassurance and next-step proof
- Blog/content = knowledge proof

## Warning

Do not overload the site with proof clutter.

Proof should increase confidence, not create noise.
