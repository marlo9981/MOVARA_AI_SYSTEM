# Trust and Authority Framework

## Core principle

Atrellis should earn trust through structure, clarity, proof, and controlled confidence.

Trust should not depend only on slogans.
It should be built into the entire experience.

## What trust should feel like

The brand should feel:
- credible
- stable
- organized
- capable
- transparent enough
- premium without being exaggerated
- modern without feeling experimental

## Where trust comes from

Trust for Atrellis should come from six major sources.

### 1. Presentation quality
A well-structured, modern, premium-looking website creates immediate confidence.

### 2. Clarity
When people understand what Atrellis does and how it fits their needs, trust increases.

### 3. Proof
Trust rises when claims are supported by examples, process, experience, category depth, suppliers, or visible capability.

### 4. Process confidence
People trust brands more when the next steps feel structured and understandable.

### 5. Reduced fragmentation
The one-stop and integrated narrative helps the brand feel more dependable and less chaotic.

### 6. Future-readiness
A digitally stronger experience can make the company feel more serious, better organized, and more prepared.

## Trust-building rule

At every important moment on the website, Atrellis should answer at least one of these questions:

- Can I trust this company?
- Do they look capable?
- Do they understand my category?
- Is this better than the usual options?
- What happens if I proceed?
- Does this feel risky or safe?

## Authority rule

Authority should be demonstrated through:
- specificity
- structure
- category understanding
- professional presentation
- proof signals
- credible associations
- useful explanations

Authority should not depend on overclaiming.

## Trust hierarchy

Use this order when planning trust signals:

1. visual and structural credibility
2. service and category clarity
3. process and next-step clarity
4. proof and reassurance
5. partner or supplier credibility
6. deeper supporting content

## Website implication

Trust should not be kept on one isolated page.

It should be distributed across:
- hero and above-the-fold sections
- service and category pages
- about and ecosystem pages
- inquiry flows
- FAQ and supporting content
