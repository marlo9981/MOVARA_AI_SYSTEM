# 05_TRUST_AND_AUTHORITY

## Purpose

This folder defines how Atrellis earns trust and builds authority.

Its role is to explain:
- why people should believe the brand,
- what forms of proof matter,
- what lowers perceived risk,
- what signals capability,
- and how reassurance should appear across the website and wider experience.

This folder is important because premium positioning only works if it is supported by visible credibility.

## What belongs here

- Trust-building framework
- Proof-point strategy
- Authority signals
- Reassurance structure
- Risk-reduction messaging
- Credibility-building principles
- What kinds of proof should appear on key pages

## What does not belong here

- Raw testimonials with no structure
- Generic claims like "quality service"
- Detailed pricing tables
- Technical implementation notes
- Long-form case study drafts without trust purpose

Those belong elsewhere.

## Usage rules

- Trust signals should be real, not inflated.
- Every major trust claim should be supportable.
- Premium perception must be backed by evidence, structure, and tone.
- Reassurance should reduce friction, not sound desperate or defensive.
