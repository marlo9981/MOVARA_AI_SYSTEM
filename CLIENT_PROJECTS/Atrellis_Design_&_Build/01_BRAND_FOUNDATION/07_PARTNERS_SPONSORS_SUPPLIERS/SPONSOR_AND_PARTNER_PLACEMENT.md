# Sponsor and Partner Placement

## Purpose

This file explains where and how partner, sponsor, and supplier references should appear across the Atrellis ecosystem.

## General placement rule

Placement should be intentional.

Do not place partner or supplier references everywhere just because they exist.

## Recommended placement logic

### Homepage
Use only the most meaningful and credibility-enhancing associations.

Purpose:
- quick confidence
- premium trust support
- ecosystem validation

### Service pages
Use category-relevant supplier or system references only where they strengthen understanding or confidence.

Purpose:
- reinforce capability
- support decision-making
- show material or system relevance

### Product or category pages
These are the strongest places for system-specific associations.

Purpose:
- increase product confidence
- show knowledge depth
- support comparison and choice

### About or ecosystem pages
Use this area to explain the relationship landscape more clearly.

Purpose:
- show seriousness
- show network quality
- provide context without cluttering conversion pages

### Inquiry and quote flow
Use associations lightly and only if they reduce hesitation.

Purpose:
- reassure
- support confidence at the point of action

## Placement warning

Do not let external brands become visual noise.

If a logo or brand mention is present, it should earn its place.

## Copy rule

When referencing a partner, sponsor, or supplier:
- keep the wording factual
- keep the relationship clear
- do not imply endorsement unless it is real
- do not exaggerate strategic significance

## Final rule

The Atrellis brand should remain the center of gravity.

External brands should strengthen the experience, not hijack it.
