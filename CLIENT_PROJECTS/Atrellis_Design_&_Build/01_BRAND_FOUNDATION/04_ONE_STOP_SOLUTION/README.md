# 04_ONE_STOP_SOLUTION

## Purpose

This folder defines how Atrellis should present itself as a one-stop solution.

Its role is to explain:
- why breadth is a strength,
- how multiple related services and systems should be framed together,
- why the in-house or integrated model matters,
- and how Atrellis can feel comprehensive without feeling messy.

This folder is important because Atrellis spans multiple service and product-system categories.

Without a clear one-stop narrative, that breadth could feel scattered instead of valuable.

## What belongs here

- One-stop brand narrative
- Integrated service logic
- In-house team advantage framing
- Design-and-build process positioning
- Convenience and coordination value
- Explanation of why breadth improves the customer experience

## What does not belong here

- Full technical service specs
- Pricing breakdowns
- Deep project ops workflows
- Generic "we do everything" claims
- Unsupported brand inflation

Those belong elsewhere.

## Source of truth

The main source of truth is:

- `ONE_STOP_SOLUTION_NARRATIVE.md`

## Usage rules

- Breadth must feel curated, not chaotic.
- Never describe Atrellis as "doing everything" with no structure.
- The one-stop idea must reduce customer friction, not just sound impressive.
- Tie the one-stop narrative to clarity, trust, coordination, and confidence.
