# 04_STAKEHOLDERS

## Purpose

This folder documents everyone involved in the Atrellis project, their roles, responsibilities, and how decisions get approved.

It ensures that any human or AI agent knows who to consult, who has final say, and how work flows through approval.

## What belongs here

- Stakeholder list and contact information
- Role definitions and responsibilities
- Approval workflows and escalation paths
- Communication protocols

## What does not belong here

- Meeting notes (go in `06_MEETINGS_AND_NOTES/`)
- Individual task assignments (go in `09_BUILD_SYSTEM/08_TASKS_AND_SPRINTS/`)
- External vendor or partner details (go in `01_BRAND_FOUNDATION/` if relevant to partnerships)

## Core files in this folder

- `STAKEHOLDERS_MASTER.md`
- `ROLES_AND_RESPONSIBILITIES.md`
- `APPROVAL_FLOW.md`
- `NOTES.md`

## Source of truth

The main source of truth is:

- `STAKEHOLDERS_MASTER.md`

## Usage rules

- Keep stakeholder information current — outdated contacts create bottlenecks
- Approval flows should be simple and explicit
- When a stakeholder's role changes, update all three files
- This folder should be readable by any AI agent brought into the project

## Relationship to other folders

- Decision authority connects to `03_DECISION_LOG/`
- Sprint and task ownership connects to `09_BUILD_SYSTEM/08_TASKS_AND_SPRINTS/`
- Client feedback and review connects to `06_MEETINGS_AND_NOTES/`
