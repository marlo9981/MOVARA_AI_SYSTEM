## What Is Atrellis?

Atrellis Design & Build is a Singapore-based design-and-build company specialising in balcony works, waterproofing, roofing systems, blinds and zip-track systems, and full home renovation. The company operates entirely in-house — from design through execution — which is its primary differentiator in a fragmented market of contractors and middlemen.

Atrellis serves homeowners and property owners who want a single, accountable partner to scope, design, and deliver their project from start to finish.

---

## What Is This Project?

This project is the design, development, and launch of the Atrellis website — built as both a premium conversion asset and a scalable platform foundation.

The website is not a brochure site. It is engineered to:
1. Convert warm traffic into qualified leads and consultation bookings
2. Build category authority through SEO/AIO content
3. Showcase Atrellis's full service range and proof-of-work
4. Lay the technical foundation for future platform features (configurators, quote tools, client portals)

---

## What Makes Atrellis Different?

- **In-house design and build** — no subcontracting the design, no fragmented ownership
- **Balcony specialists** — a defined niche within renovation that carries specific credibility
- **Product-backed services** — partners with trusted brands like Nippon Paint; sells named product systems (zipblinds, ziptrack)
- **One-stop solution** — design, supply, install, warranty under one roof
