# Website and Platform Objectives
**Status:** Draft — to be reviewed and confirmed
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-05

---

## Primary Business Objectives

These are the outcomes Atrellis needs from this project — not features, but business results.

1. **Generate qualified leads** — The website must convert warm traffic into consultation requests and inquiry submissions from homeowners and property owners in Singapore.
2. **Build category authority** — Atrellis should rank for and be returned in AI-generated answers for searches related to balcony works, waterproofing, zipblinds, roofing systems, and full renovation.
3. **Reduce sales friction** — The website should pre-qualify leads, communicate pricing context, and give visitors enough confidence to make contact without needing a phone call first.
4. **Showcase proof of work** — Project galleries and case studies must make Atrellis's quality and range visible to every visitor.

---

## Website Objectives (Specific)

| Objective | Measured By |
|---|---|
| Visitor → Lead conversion | Form submissions, consultation bookings |
| Search visibility | Ranking positions, AI Overview appearances |
| Trust signal delivery | Time on page, return visits, testimonial engagement |
| Service clarity | Bounce rate per service page, scroll depth |
| Mobile performance | Core Web Vitals, mobile conversion rate |

---

## Platform Objectives (Future)

The website is built to grow into a platform. The objectives for later phases include:

- **Automated quoting** — reduce the time between inquiry and proposal
- **Client portal** — give clients access to project status, documents, and approvals
- **Configurator** — let users explore options (blinds type, roofing system) before speaking to sales
- **Internal lead dashboard** — give the Atrellis team visibility over pipeline and conversion

---

## What Success Looks Like at Launch

> To be defined with project lead. Draft targets below:

- Site live and fully indexed within 2 weeks of launch
- All core service pages ranking within 90 days
- First qualified inbound lead within 30 days of launch
- No critical CWV failures at launch
