\# Movara AI – Positioning and ICP

\#\# Positioning Statement  
Movara AI builds high-conversion websites and workflow automations for growth-stage businesses in Southeast Asia who need their digital presence to drive measurable revenue, not just look good.

\*\*Unlike\*\*: Generic web agencies that deliver static sites    
\*\*We\*\*: Combine conversion-optimized UI with integrated automation workflows that capture leads, nurture prospects, and close deals while you sleep.

\#\# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

\#\#\# Firmographics  
\- \*\*Company Size\*\*: 10–100 employees  
\- \*\*Revenue\*\*: $500K–$10M ARR  
\- \*\*Geography\*\*: Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Philippines  
\- \*\*Industries\*\*: SaaS, E-commerce, Professional Services, Hospitality, Real Estate

\#\#\# Psychographics  
\- \*\*Pain\*\*: Website doesn't convert, too many manual sales/ops processes  
\- \*\*Goal\*\*: Scale revenue without scaling headcount linearly  
\- \*\*Budget\*\*: $5K–$50K for website \+ automation project  
\- \*\*Decision-maker\*\*: Founder, CMO, Head of Ops

\#\#\# Buyer Persona: "Growth-Blocked Founder"  
\- Age: 30–45  
\- Role: Founder / Co-founder  
\- Challenges:  
  \- Current website is a brochure, not a revenue engine  
  \- Sales team drowning in manual lead qualification  
  \- No clear pipeline visibility or follow-up automation  
\- Desired Outcome:  
  \- Website that pre-qualifies leads via interactive tools  
  \- Automated nurture sequences triggered by user behavior  
  \- Dashboard showing lead quality, conversion rates, ROI

\#\# Anti-ICP (Who We Don't Serve)  
\- Startups with \<$100K revenue (too early)  
\- Enterprises with \>500 employees (too complex, procurement friction)  
\- Clients who want "just a logo" or "just a landing page" with no conversion strategy

\#\# Positioning Pillars

\#\#\# 1\. Conversion Engineering  
Every pixel, interaction, and workflow designed to move prospects down the funnel.

\#\#\# 2\. Automation-First  
We don't just build sites—we build systems that qualify, nurture, and close leads automatically.

\#\#\# 3\. SEA Market Expertise  
Pricing, case studies, compliance, and UX localized for Southeast Asian buyers.
