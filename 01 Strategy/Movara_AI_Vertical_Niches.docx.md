\# Movara AI – Vertical Niches

\#\# Niche Selection Criteria  
1\. \*\*High Intent\*\*: Industry has urgent need for lead gen \+ automation  
2\. \*\*Budget Fit\*\*: Typical deal size $10K–$50K per project  
3\. \*\*Scalable Delivery\*\*: Repeatable workflows, templatizable components  
4\. \*\*SEA Presence\*\*: Strong market activity in target geographies

\#\# Priority Verticals

\#\#\# 1\. SaaS & Tech Startups  
\*\*Why\*\*: Need to scale demos, trials, onboarding without scaling headcount    
\*\*Pain\*\*: Manual lead qualification, slow demo scheduling, leaky trial-to-paid funnel    
\*\*Solution\*\*: Interactive product demo embeds, automated trial nurture, usage-based scoring    
\*\*Example\*\*: B2B HR tech platform with gated feature calculator → auto-books sales calls

\#\#\# 2\. Real Estate & Property Development  
\*\*Why\*\*: High ticket, visual-first, need to pre-qualify serious buyers    
\*\*Pain\*\*: Unqualified inquiries, site visits waste time, no pipeline visibility    
\*\*Solution\*\*: Floorplan configurators, mortgage calculators, gated brochure downloads, auto lead scoring    
\*\*Example\*\*: Condo developer with 3D unit selector → captures budget, timeline, contact before sales call

\#\#\# 3\. Renovation & Home Services (Atrellis playbook)  
\*\*Why\*\*: Complex quoting, visual customization, high CAC    
\*\*Pain\*\*: Manual quotes take days, showroom visits required, low conversion from web    
\*\*Solution\*\*: Product configurators (blinds, flooring, kitchens), instant quote generation, gated renders    
\*\*Example\*\*: Renovation platform with drag-drop floorplan → generates itemized quote \+ books consultation

\#\#\# 4\. Professional Services (Legal, Accounting, Consulting)  
\*\*Why\*\*: Need to demonstrate expertise, filter low-value inquiries    
\*\*Pain\*\*: Generic "Contact Us" forms, no lead qualification, pricing opacity    
\*\*Solution\*\*: Interactive ROI calculators, diagnostic quizzes, gated industry reports    
\*\*Example\*\*: Tax consulting firm with tax-savings estimator → captures company size, revenue, books discovery

\#\#\# 5\. Hospitality & F\&B  
\*\*Why\*\*: Need reservations, event bookings, catering inquiries automated    
\*\*Pain\*\*: Phone/email tag for bookings, no upsell automation, scattered customer data    
\*\*Solution\*\*: Booking widgets, event quote configurators, automated email nurture, loyalty triggers    
\*\*Example\*\*: Restaurant group with event planner tool → auto-quotes menu packages, books site visits
