# Movara AI — Business Plan

## Mission

Empower organizations to leverage advanced AI solutions that enhance decision-making, automate critical workflows, and drive sustainable competitive advantage through accessible, intelligent technology.

## Vision

To become the leading provider of enterprise AI platforms that democratize access to cutting-edge artificial intelligence, enabling businesses of all sizes to innovate faster and scale more efficiently.

## Target Market

Mid-market to enterprise organizations across financial services, healthcare, e-commerce, and professional services sectors seeking to improve operational efficiency and customer engagement through AI-driven insights and automation.

## Revenue Model

SaaS subscription tiers ($5K-$50K/month) based on usage and features; custom enterprise implementations (15-25% of revenue); professional services and consulting (10-15% of revenue); API access and third-party integrations.

## 90-Day Goals

- Launch MVP with core AI engine and dashboard interface; achieve 500+ beta signups
- Establish partnerships with 3-5 key technology integrators and consulting firms
- Develop customer success playbooks and onboarding documentation
- Secure initial customer advisory board of 10+ enterprise practitioners
- Complete Series A fundraising round ($3-5M) to scale engineering and go-to-market teams
