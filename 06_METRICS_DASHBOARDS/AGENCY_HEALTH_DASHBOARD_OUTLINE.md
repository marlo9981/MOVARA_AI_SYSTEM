\# Agency Health Dashboard Outline

Purpose: high-level view of Movara AI health across pipeline, delivery, and finances.

\#\# Sections

1\. Pipeline Health  
\- Number of active opportunities (by stage).  
\- Total pipeline value.  
\- Expected revenue this quarter.

2\. Revenue and Cash  
\- Monthly booked revenue.  
\- Collected vs outstanding invoices.  
\- Revenue by vertical (Real Estate, Renovation, etc.).

3\. Delivery Load  
\- Active projects and their stages.  
\- Estimated capacity vs actual load.  
\- On-time vs at-risk deliveries.

4\. Quality and Satisfaction  
\- Average client satisfaction score.  
\- Number of referrals.  
\- Repeat work percentage.

5\. Strategic Metrics  
\- Vertical mix (how much of pipeline and revenue is from chosen niches).  
\- Retainer vs project ratio.  
\- Any key experiments (e.g. new offer performance).

\#\# Implementation Notes

\- Start as a simple Google Sheet or Notion table.  
\- Update cadence: monthly review, with a quick weekly snapshot.  
\- Use the definitions from \`Core\_KPIs\_and\_Definitions.md\`, \`Sales\_and\_Funnel\_Metrics.md\`, and \`Delivery\_and\_Quality\_Metrics.md\` to keep everything consistent.
