\# Delivery and Quality Metrics

Purpose: track how well Movara AI delivers projects once sold.

\#\# Key Areas

\- Timeliness  
\- Scope control  
\- Quality and rework  
\- Client satisfaction

\#\# Key Metrics

1\. On-Time Delivery Rate  
\- Definition: percentage of projects delivered on or before agreed launch date.  
\- Formula: projects\_on\_time / total\_projects.

2\. Scope Creep Incidents  
\- Definition: number of projects where scope expanded without matching budget change.  
\- Track as: count per project \+ reasons.

3\. Revision Cycles per Project  
\- Definition: number of major revision rounds needed.  
\- Use to spot patterns in scoping or expectation setting.

4\. Client Satisfaction Score  
\- Definition: simple 1–5 or 1–10 rating after project.  
\- Track per project and by vertical.

5\. Repeat / Expansion Work  
\- Definition: percentage of clients who come back for additional work or retainers.  
\- Signals long-term value and fit.

\#\# Notes

\- Start simple; a lightweight spreadsheet is enough initially.  
\- Over time, map these into a proper dashboard or reporting tool.
