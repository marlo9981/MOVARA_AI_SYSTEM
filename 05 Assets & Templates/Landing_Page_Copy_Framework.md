\# Landing Page Copy Framework

Purpose: reusable structure for Movara AI or client landing pages focused on one clear offer.

\#\# 1\. Hero Section

Elements:  
\- Main headline: clear outcome or transformation  
\- Subheadline: who it’s for \+ how it works (1–2 lines)  
\- Primary CTA: what happens next (book, request, calculate, etc.)  
\- Optional: short trust signal (clients, results, vertical)

Questions to answer:  
\- What is the main promise?  
\- Who is this specifically for?  
\- What is the primary action?

\#\# 2\. Problem / Pain Section

Goal: show you understand their world.

Content:  
\- 2–4 bullets describing their current pains  
\- Short paragraph summarising the situation in their language  
\- Optional: small visual or statistic

\#\# 3\. Solution Overview

Goal: introduce how the offer works at a high level.

Content:  
\- Short intro paragraph: what Movara AI does for this use case  
\- 3–4 key pillars/features in bullet or card format  
\- Each pillar should tie to outcomes (not just features)

\#\# 4\. How It Works (Steps)

Goal: make the process feel simple and concrete.

Content:  
\- 3–5 steps (e.g. “1. Discovery”, “2. Design”, “3. Build”, “4. Automate”, “5. Optimise”)  
\- Each step: 1–2 sentences, focusing on what the client experiences

\#\# 5\. Proof and Credibility

Goal: reduce risk perception.

Content:  
\- Selected stats, logos, testimonials, or case study snippets  
\- If early: focus on patterns and experience instead of big names

\#\# 6\. Offer Details / What You Get

Goal: show what is included in plain language.

Content:  
\- Bullet list of concrete deliverables:  
  \- Pages or flows  
  \- Automations  
  \- Integrations  
  \- Support  
\- Align this list with the relevant package (Basic/Premium/Enterprise)

\#\# 7\. Pricing and Fit

Goal: set expectations and self-qualify.

Content:  
\- Simple pricing language (range or “starting from” is fine)  
\- Short list: “Who this is for” and “Who this is not for”  
\- Clarify where this sits vs other packages if needed

\#\# 8\. FAQ

Goal: pre-handle common objections.

Content:  
\- 4–6 questions and answers:  
  \- Timeline  
  \- Process  
  \- What’s required from them  
  \- Tool choices  
  \- Result expectations

\#\# 9\. Final CTA

Goal: reinforce the action.

Content:  
\- Short recap of value  
\- Clear CTA button or link  
\- Optional: secondary lower-commitment option (e.g. download, watch walkthrough)
