\# Email Follow-up Sequences

Purpose: reusable email flows for post-call, post-proposal, and “no response” scenarios.

\#\# 1\. After Discovery Call (Warm Fit)

\*\*Email 1 – Same Day Recap\*\*

Subject: Great speaking today, quick recap

Hi {{first\_name}},

Thanks again for taking the time to walk through your current setup and goals today.

Here’s a quick recap of what I heard:  
\- {{point\_1}}  
\- {{point\_2}}  
\- {{point\_3}}

Based on that, the next step is for me to draft a {{proposal\_type}} that covers:  
\- {{scope\_summary}}  
\- {{timeline\_summary}}  
\- {{outcome\_summary}}

You can expect that by {{date}}. In the meantime, if you remember anything important we missed, just reply here.

Best,    
{{sender\_name}}

\---

\*\*Email 2 – Proposal Sent Reminder (2–3 days after sending)\*\*

Subject: Any questions on the Movara AI proposal?

Hi {{first\_name}},

Just checking in to see if you had a chance to review the proposal I sent on {{date}}.

If it’s helpful, we can jump on a quick call to walk through:  
\- The approach  
\- Scope boundaries  
\- Budget and payment structure

Would a short {{15/20}} minute call sometime this week help move this forward?

Best,    
{{sender\_name}}

\---

\#\# 2\. After Proposal – No Response

\*\*Email 1 – Light Nudge (3–5 days)\*\*

Subject: Quick check-in on the proposal

Hi {{first\_name}},

Hope things are going well on your side.

Just wanted to check in on the proposal for {{project\_name}} – is this still on your radar or has priority shifted for now?

If it’s still relevant, happy to:  
\- Clarify anything that’s unclear  
\- Explore a phased or lighter approach  
\- Adjust timelines to fit your internal constraints

If it’s not the right time, that’s totally fine too – a quick reply either way helps me plan.

Best,    
{{sender\_name}}

\---

\*\*Email 2 – Close-the-loop (10–14 days)\*\*

Subject: Should we pause this for now?

Hi {{first\_name}},

I haven’t heard back on the proposal, so I’ll assume timing might not be ideal right now.

Would you like to:  
\- Put this on hold and revisit later, or  
\- Close it out for now and reconnect when things change?

Either way, no pressure – I just want to keep my side organised.

Best,    
{{sender\_name}}

\---

\#\# 3\. After “Not Now” / Lost for Timing

\*\*Email – Keep-the-door-open\*\*

Subject: Appreciate the update

Hi {{first\_name}},

Thanks for letting me know about the timing on {{project\_name}}.

I completely understand – these projects are easiest when there’s enough space and attention for them.

If it’s useful, I can:  
\- Send an occasional update when we publish new case studies or examples, and  
\- Check in again around {{month/quarter}} to see if anything has changed.

In the meantime, if anything shifts earlier, just reply here and we can pick it up.

Best,    
{{sender\_name}}
