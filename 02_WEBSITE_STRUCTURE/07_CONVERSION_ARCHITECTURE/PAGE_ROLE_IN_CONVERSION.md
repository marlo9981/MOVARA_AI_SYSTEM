# Page Role in Conversion

## Purpose

This file explains what role each major page group plays in the conversion system.

## Home

Main role:
- attention
- understanding
- first-level trust
- routing

The homepage should not try to do everything.
Its main job is to orient and route well.

## Services

Main role:
- understanding
- relevance
- one-stop interpretation

The services area helps broad visitors identify fit and move into the right area.

## Products / Systems

Main role:
- specific relevance
- category confidence
- direct-intent support

These pages are often closer to action because intent is more specific.

## Projects / Blog

Main role:
- authority
- education
- trust support
- relevance expansion

These pages often support conversion indirectly by increasing confidence.

## About

Main role:
- trust
- brand interpretation
- reassurance

The about area matters most for serious evaluators.

## Contact

Main role:
- action
- friction-managed conversion
- transition into follow-up

The contact area should make action feel easy and worthwhile.

## Supporting rule

A page should be judged partly by how well it supports the larger conversion system, not only by isolated direct conversion rates.
