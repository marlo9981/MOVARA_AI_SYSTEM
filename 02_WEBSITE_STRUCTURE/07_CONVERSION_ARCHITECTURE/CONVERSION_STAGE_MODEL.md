# Conversion Stage Model

## Purpose

This file defines the main stages of conversion across the Atrellis website.

## Stage 1: Attention

The visitor notices Atrellis.

Main needs:
- clean first impression
- immediate clarity
- premium and credible feel

Main page contributors:
- Home
- category landing pages
- selected content entry pages

---

## Stage 2: Understanding

The visitor starts to understand what Atrellis is and what it offers.

Main needs:
- simple explanation
- controlled breadth
- clear category grouping

Main page contributors:
- Home
- Services
- Products / Systems

---

## Stage 3: Relevance

The visitor sees that Atrellis may fit their specific need.

Main needs:
- category match
- service relevance
- clearer use-case fit

Main page contributors:
- service pages
- product/system pages
- specific content pages

---

## Stage 4: Trust

The visitor feels more confident that Atrellis is credible.

Main needs:
- authority signals
- process reassurance
- proof and brand confidence
- less generic presentation

Main page contributors:
- About
- Projects / Blog
- proof-support sections
- partner/supplier trust layers

---

## Stage 5: Confidence

The visitor feels ready enough to take the next step.

Main needs:
- reduced hesitation
- clear next-step expectation
- low-friction path forward

Main page contributors:
- CTA zones
- inquiry support areas
- contact page
- category-specific action modules

---

## Stage 6: Action

The visitor submits an inquiry or enters a more structured lead path.

Main needs:
- clear action framing
- justified form friction
- confidence that something useful happens next

Main page contributors:
- Contact
- embedded inquiry modules
- future guided flows

## Stage rule

Not every page must perform every stage equally.

The website works best when different pages work together across these stages.
