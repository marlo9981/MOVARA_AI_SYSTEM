MOVARA AI – DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT AND NOTES

CALL OBJECTIVE  
\- Confirm fit and value.  
\- Understand the current situation and desired outcomes.  
\- Decide clear next steps (proposal, deeper scoping, or no fit).

STRUCTURE (30–45 MINUTES)

1\. Opening (3–5 minutes)  
\- Quick intro and context.  
\- Set agenda:  
  “We’ll spend a bit of time understanding your current setup and goals, then walk through how Movara AI might help, and if it makes sense, we can outline next steps.”

2\. Context and Current State (10–15 minutes)  
Key prompts:  
\- “What made you reach out now?”  
\- “How are you currently getting leads?”  
\- “What role does your website play today?”  
\- “What is painful or frustrating in your current process?”

3\. Desired Outcomes (5–10 minutes)  
Key prompts:  
\- “If this goes really well, what would change in the next 6–12 months?”  
\- “What would a ‘win’ look like for you?”  
\- “Are there specific metrics you care about? (lead volume, quality, response time, etc.)”

4\. Constraints and Buying Process (5–10 minutes)  
Key prompts:  
\- “Who else needs to be involved in the decision?”  
\- “How are you thinking about budget for this?”  
\- “Is there any internal timing we should be aware of?”

5\. High-Level Fit Check and Next Steps (5–10 minutes)  
\- Summarize what you heard.  
\- Give a high-level explanation of how Movara AI could help.  
\- Suggest the next step (proposal, demo, or second call with stakeholders).
