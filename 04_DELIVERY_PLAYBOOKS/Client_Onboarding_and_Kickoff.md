MOVARA AI – CLIENT ONBOARDING AND KICKOFF

PURPOSE  
Make the transition from “closed-won” to “in delivery” smooth, clear, and confident for the client.

ONBOARDING CHECKLIST (INTERNAL)  
\- Contract signed and deposit received.  
\- Create project folder and shared workspace.  
\- Confirm main client contact and stakeholders.  
\- Schedule kickoff call.  
\- Send welcome and expectations email.

CLIENT WELCOME EMAIL OUTLINE  
\- Thank them and confirm that the project is starting.  
\- Introduce key people and roles.  
\- Share what will happen in the next 1–2 weeks.  
\- Attach or link to the onboarding questionnaire and checklist.  
\- Confirm kickoff call date and agenda.

KICKOFF CALL AGENDA (60–90 MINUTES)  
\- Quick intro and recap of goals.  
\- Walk through project phases and timeline.  
\- Review onboarding questionnaire answers.  
\- Clarify priorities, risks, and unknowns.  
\- Agree on communication channels and cadence.  
\- Confirm next immediate actions on both sides.

ONBOARDING QUESTIONNAIRE (EXAMPLE SECTIONS)  
\- Business overview  
\- Existing website and tools  
\- Audience and key segments  
\- Offers and services  
\- Current lead flow and bottlenecks  
\- Success metrics and constraints

NOTES  
\- Keep onboarding as lightweight as possible but no lighter.  
\- Remove anything that does not help delivery or clarity.
