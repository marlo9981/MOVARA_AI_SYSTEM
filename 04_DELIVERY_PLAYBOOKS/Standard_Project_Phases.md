MOVARA AI – STANDARD PROJECT PHASES

OVERVIEW  
Typical full project:  
1\. Onboarding and Discovery  
2\. Experience Design (UX/UI)  
3\. Build and Integrations  
4\. Automation and Workflows  
5\. QA, Launch, and Handoff

PHASE 1 – ONBOARDING AND DISCOVERY  
Goals:  
\- Align on goals, scope, and success metrics.  
\- Gather all necessary assets and access.

Typical steps:  
\- Send welcome email and onboarding checklist.  
\- Collect access (domains, existing tools, analytics, CRM).  
\- Run a kickoff workshop or call.  
\- Document goals, constraints, and must-haves.

PHASE 2 – EXPERIENCE DESIGN (UX/UI)  
Goals:  
\- Design the user journeys, structure, and main screens.  
\- Get visual alignment before heavy build.

Typical steps:  
\- Sitemap and key flows.  
\- Wireframes for core pages and tools.  
\- Visual design concepts.  
\- Design review and sign-off.

PHASE 3 – BUILD AND INTEGRATIONS  
Goals:  
\- Implement approved designs in code.  
\- Connect to database and basic integrations.

Typical steps:  
\- Frontend development.  
\- Database schema and API setup.  
\- Basic integrations (analytics, CRM, email tools).  
\- Internal QA and review.

PHASE 4 – AUTOMATION AND WORKFLOWS  
Goals:  
\- Build the automations that turn the site into a system.

Typical steps:  
\- Map trigger points (forms, tool interactions, events).  
\- Build automations (lead routing, notifications, nurture).  
\- Test flows end-to-end.  
\- Document logic for client.

PHASE 5 – QA, LAUNCH, AND HANDOFF  
Goals:  
\- Ensure quality, then launch and hand control over cleanly.

Typical steps:  
\- Cross-device and browser QA.  
\- Content and data checks.  
\- Launch checklist and go-live.  
\- Training session and handoff docs.
