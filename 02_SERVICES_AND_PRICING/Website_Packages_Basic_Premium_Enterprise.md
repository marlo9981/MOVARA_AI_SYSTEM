WEBSITE PACKAGES – BASIC / PREMIUM / ENTERPRISE

PURPOSE  
These packages define the main website build offers for Movara AI. They should be:  
\- Easy to explain in a sales conversation  
\- Clearly differentiated  
\- Able to map onto different ICP segments and budgets

OVERVIEW TABLE (HIGH LEVEL)

Basic  
\- Goal: get a focused, conversion-aware website live quickly.  
\- For: smaller teams or early-stage ICP clients.

Premium  
\- Goal: build a richer site with more sections, assets, and polish.  
\- For: growth-stage ICP clients who want a strong digital front door.

Enterprise  
\- Goal: support complex navigation, multiple segments, or advanced features.  
\- For: larger or more complex organisations.

────────────────────────────────  
BASIC WEBSITE PACKAGE  
────────────────────────────────

Who it’s for  
\- Businesses that need a strong but focused website.  
\- Usually 3–6 core pages and a clear primary action.

Scope (example)  
\- Pages: Home, About, Services/Offer, Contact, \+1–2 supporting pages.  
\- Content: based on client input and light guidance.  
\- Design: one primary layout system, responsive for mobile and desktop.  
\- Forms: basic contact or enquiry form integrated with CRM or email.

Key characteristics  
\- Fast to launch.  
\- Focused on one main ICP or segment.  
\- Straightforward navigation.

What’s not included (by default)  
\- Complex content libraries or blogs.  
\- Multi-language setups.  
\- Advanced interactive tools (unless added as an option).

────────────────────────────────  
PREMIUM WEBSITE PACKAGE  
────────────────────────────────

Who it’s for  
\- Growth-stage businesses that need more depth, content, and credibility.  
\- More than one ICP or use case to communicate.

Scope (example)  
\- Pages: Home, About, Services (broken into sections or pages), Case Studies, Resources or Blog, Contact, and 1–2 campaign or landing pages.  
\- Design: more advanced visual system, more components, richer interactions.  
\- Content: more involvement in structuring messaging and story.  
\- Forms: multiple lead capture points (general, specific offers, gated content).  
\- Optional: one simple interactive element (e.g. mini-calculator or quiz) as part of the package.

Key characteristics  
\- Stronger positioning and storytelling.  
\- Multiple entry points for different ICPs.  
\- Ready to plug into automation packages.

What’s not included (by default)  
\- Very complex custom applications.  
\- Deep multi-language or multi-region logic.  
\- Heavy custom integrations outside normal stack.

────────────────────────────────  
ENTERPRISE WEBSITE PACKAGE  
────────────────────────────────

Who it’s for  
\- Larger organisations or complex products.  
\- Multiple segments, product lines, or regions.

Scope (example)  
\- Pages: full sitemap structure, including product lines, industries/verticals, resources, case studies, careers, support, etc.  
\- Design: fully bespoke design system, more complex grids and components.  
\- Content: guidance and collaboration on messaging for different segments.  
\- Features: can include:  
  \- Advanced interactive tools  
  \- Role- or segment-based journeys  
  \- Deeper integration with back-office tools

Key characteristics  
\- Designed as a platform, not just a small site.  
\- Supports multiple stakeholder journeys.  
\- Built to be extended and maintained over time.

What’s not included (by default)  
\- Completely custom web apps that are outside marketing/sales scope (can be separate projects).  
\- Non-standard or extremely niche integrations without prior agreement.

NOTES AND PRICING DIRECTION  
────────────────────────────────

\- Each tier should have a clear starting price and a typical range.  
\- The main differences between tiers:  
  \- Number and complexity of pages.  
  \- Depth of design system.  
  \- Amount of content support.  
  \- Number and complexity of interactive elements.  
\- Over time, add real examples per tier (client, sector, and approximate project size).
