\# Movara AI – Service Catalog Overview

\#\# Core Offerings

\#\#\# 1\. High-Conversion Websites

Full-stack web applications designed to capture, qualify, and convert leads.

\*\*Includes\*\*:

\- Conversion-optimized UI/UX design

\- Interactive configurators, calculators, or diagnostic tools

\- Lead capture and database integration

\- Mobile-responsive, fast-loading (Next.js \+ Vercel)

\- Analytics and heatmap tracking setup

\*\*Tiers\*\*: Basic, Premium, Enterprise

\---

\#\#\# 2\. Workflow Automation

Integrated systems that automate lead nurture, sales handoff, and customer onboarding.

\*\*Includes\*\*:

\- Lead scoring and segmentation

\- Email/SMS nurture sequences

\- CRM integration (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive)

\- Automated appointment booking

\- Slack/Teams notifications for sales team

\*\*Tiers\*\*: Starter, Growth, Enterprise

\---

\#\#\# 3\. Interactive Product Configurators

Custom tools for high-ticket or complex products (real estate, renovation, SaaS).

\*\*Examples\*\*:

\- Floorplan selector with pricing

\- Renovation quote generator (materials \+ labor)

\- SaaS feature comparison with custom quote

\*\*Pricing\*\*: $3K–$15K depending on complexity

\---

\#\#\# 4\. Lead Magnets & Gated Assets

High-value tools that capture contact info in exchange for personalized outputs.

\*\*Examples\*\*:

\- ROI calculators

\- Diagnostic quizzes (e.g., "What's your website conversion score?")

\- Downloadable reports (gated behind form)

\*\*Pricing\*\*: $1K–$5K per asset
