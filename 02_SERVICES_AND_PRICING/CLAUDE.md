\# Services and Pricing Folder Guidelines

\#\# Purpose  
Define WHAT we sell, HOW we package it, and at WHAT price points.

\#\# Documents in This Folder  
1\. \*\*Service\_Catalog\_Overview.docx\*\*: Master list of all offerings  
2\. \*\*Website\_Packages\_Basic\_Premium\_Enterprise.docx\*\*: Tiered website builds

\#\# Pricing Philosophy  
\- \*\*Transparent\*\*: No "contact us"—publish tiers upfront  
\- \*\*Value-Based\*\*: Priced on client outcome (revenue lift), not hours  
\- \*\*SEA-Calibrated\*\*: Competitive with local agencies, premium vs DIY tools  
\- \*\*Packaged\*\*: Fixed scope reduces negotiation friction

\#\# Update Trigger  
Revise when:  
\- Market feedback shows price resistance or underpricing  
\- New service capabilities emerge (e.g., AI chatbot add-on)  
\- Competitive pricing shifts require repositioning
