  
CLAUDE GUIDELINES – 05\_ASSETS\_TEMPLATES

PURPOSE OF THIS FOLDER  
This folder holds reusable assets and templates for Movara AI: proposals, email sequences, messaging, decks, and anything that should not be reinvented each time.

TYPICAL CONTENTS  
\- Proposal\_Template  
\- Email\_Followup\_Sequences  
\- Case\_Study\_Outline  
\- Landing\_Page\_Copy\_Framework  
\- Outreach\_Message\_Scripts  
\- MCP\_INTEGRATIONS/ (MCP stack status, model routing guide, multi-model workflows)

GENERAL RULES  
1\. Templates should be practical and ready to customize.  
2\. Avoid over-complication; keep the structure simple and repeatable.  
3\. Add examples over time, but keep one master template per asset type.
