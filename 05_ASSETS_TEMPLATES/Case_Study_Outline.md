\# Case Study Outline

Purpose: standard structure for Movara AI case studies.

\#\# 1\. Headline Snapshot

\- Client name (or anonymised)  
\- Industry / vertical  
\- Region  
\- Short outcome headline (e.g. “3× more qualified leads in 90 days”)

\#\# 2\. About the Client

\- Who they are  
\- What they do  
\- Basic size (team / revenue band if possible)  
\- Where they operate (country/region)

\#\# 3\. The Challenge

\- What was not working with their website and workflows  
\- Symptoms (e.g. low conversions, manual follow-up, poor data)  
\- Why this mattered now (timing, growth stage, market pressure)

\#\# 4\. The Solution

Break into 2 parts:

\*\*Website / Experience\*\*

\- What changed on the website (structure, messaging, pages, tools)  
\- Any notable UX or conversion improvements

\*\*Automation / Workflows\*\*

\- What automations were implemented (lead capture, scoring, nurture, routing)  
\- Tools integrated (CRM, email, calendars, etc.)

\#\# 5\. Implementation Highlights

\- How long it took (rough timeline)  
\- Any notable constraints or challenges  
\- How collaboration with the client worked (calls, reviews, approvals)

\#\# 6\. Results

\- Quantitative metrics (before vs after), if available:  
  \- Conversion rate change  
  \- Lead volume and/or quality change  
  \- Response time improvements  
\- Qualitative:  
  \- Client feedback  
  \- Internal team impact (e.g. less manual follow-up)

\#\# 7\. Key Learnings

\- What made this project successful  
\- What might be done differently next time  
\- How this informs Movara AI playbooks for this vertical

\#\# 8\. Call-to-Action (Public-Facing Version)

\- A short closing paragraph aimed at similar prospects  
\- Clear next step (e.g. “Book a call”, “See how this approach applies to your business”)
