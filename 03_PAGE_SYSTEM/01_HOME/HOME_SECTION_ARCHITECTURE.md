# Home Section Architecture

## Recommended homepage section flow

### 1. Hero
Purpose:
- immediate brand impression
- broad value proposition
- entry into key next steps

Needs:
- strong clarity
- premium feel
- clean CTA hierarchy

### 2. Trust / differentiation layer
Purpose:
- quickly show why Atrellis feels stronger than the category norm
- increase confidence early

Needs:
- proof-oriented framing
- controlled trust signals
- clear differentiators

### 3. Broad services / systems overview
Purpose:
- show breadth without chaos
- help users see what Atrellis covers

Needs:
- grouped categories
- clean structure
- links into relevant deeper pages

### 4. One-stop / process narrative
Purpose:
- show that breadth is an advantage
- explain why the experience is more organized and easier to move through

Needs:
- process clarity
- confidence-building framing
- lower-friction interpretation

### 5. Projects / proof / authority layer
Purpose:
- build confidence through examples, work, or authority content

Needs:
- visual proof
- selected highlights
- links to deeper proof content

### 6. About / ecosystem / brand trust layer
Purpose:
- reinforce credibility
- show seriousness and structure

Needs:
- concise company framing
- relevant trust support
- not too much detail

### 7. Contact / inquiry / next-step section
Purpose:
- give action-ready users a clear way forward

Needs:
- low-friction CTA
- clear expectation
- strong but calm closing action

## Section rule

The homepage should move users through this rhythm:

impression -> trust -> understanding -> relevance -> confidence -> action
