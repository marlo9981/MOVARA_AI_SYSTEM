# Home Master

## Core role

The homepage should give visitors a premium, clear, and confidence-building first impression of Atrellis.

It should help users quickly understand:
- what Atrellis is
- what kinds of needs it covers
- why it feels different
- and where to go next

## Main jobs of the homepage

The homepage should:

- establish brand perception
- communicate broad value quickly
- support trust early
- show controlled service and system breadth
- route users toward relevant deeper pages
- create a clear path into inquiry or next-step exploration

## What the homepage should not do

It should not:
- try to explain every service in full
- become a crowded category dump
- rely only on aesthetics without clarity
- push a hard-sell CTA too early for every user

## Homepage success criteria

A good Atrellis homepage should make the user feel:

- this looks premium
- this seems well organized
- this company feels more trustworthy than typical options
- I understand where to go next
- this might fit what I need

## Homepage strategic role

The homepage is not the entire website.

Its main function is to:
- orient,
- position,
- reassure,
- and route.
