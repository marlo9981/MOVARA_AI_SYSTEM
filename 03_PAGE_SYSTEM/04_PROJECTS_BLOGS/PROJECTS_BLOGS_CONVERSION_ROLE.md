# Projects Blogs Conversion Role

## Core role

Projects and blogs often support conversion indirectly before they support it directly.

## Main conversion outcomes

These pages should move users toward:
- stronger trust
- deeper relevance
- related service or product pages
- inquiry readiness

## Best CTA logic

Typical CTA patterns:
- Explore related service
- See similar solutions
- Ask about your project
- Learn how this applies to your space

## Conversion rule

Do not measure these pages only by immediate direct-contact submissions.

Their value often includes:
- authority building
- organic discovery
- confidence increase
- better user qualification

## Strategic rule

Projects and blogs should hand off to action cleanly, but without sounding forced or interruptive.
