# Authority Content Logic

## Core principle

Projects and blogs should strengthen authority in two different but connected ways.

## Projects role

Projects should provide:
- proof
- taste
- capability signals
- quality perception
- confidence through examples

## Blogs role

Blogs should provide:
- education
- search visibility
- answer-oriented content
- category understanding
- buyer guidance

## Combined value

Together, projects and blogs help Atrellis feel:
- experienced
- thoughtful
- trustworthy
- more serious than low-effort competitors

## Routing rule

Projects and blogs should connect back to:
- related services
- related products / systems
- relevant inquiry paths

## Quality rule

Not every content asset deserves publication.

Only publish content that improves:
- authority
- clarity
- relevance
- or conversion support
