# Service Landing Master

## Core role

The services landing page should help users understand the breadth of Atrellis in a structured and confidence-building way.

It should answer:
- what Atrellis covers
- how services are grouped
- where the visitor should go next
- why the one-stop model is useful

## Main jobs of the services page

The services page should:

- translate broad offer range into clear structure
- support one-stop positioning
- guide users into more specific service areas
- build confidence that breadth does not mean chaos
- support both relevance discovery and action

## What the page should not do

It should not:
- present every service equally with no hierarchy
- become a long unstructured list
- duplicate every service-detail page in full
- rely on generic category naming with no explanation

## Success criteria

A good services page should make the user feel:

- they cover what I might need
- this is easier to understand than most competitors
- they seem capable across related areas
- I know where to click next
