# Service Grouping Logic

## Core principle

Services should be grouped in a way that makes Atrellis feel broad but intentional.

Grouping should improve understanding, not just organize a list.

## Recommended grouping logic

Use a small number of clear groupings, such as:

- renovation and design-build services
- outdoor and balcony-related systems
- roofing and weather-protection systems
- blinds and enclosure-related systems
- repair, upgrade, or support-related categories where relevant

## Grouping rule

Each group should:
- feel internally coherent
- have a clear label
- have a short explanatory layer
- link into more specific pages

## Why grouping matters

Good grouping helps:
- reduce overwhelm
- increase relevance
- reinforce one-stop positioning
- make navigation easier

## Warning

Do not create too many top-level service groups too early.

Broad categories should stay manageable and easy to scan.
