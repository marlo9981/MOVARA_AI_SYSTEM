CLAUDE GUIDELINES – 04\_DELIVERY\_PLAYBOOKS

PURPOSE OF THIS FOLDER  
This folder defines how Movara AI delivers projects: onboarding, discovery, design, build, automation, launch, and handoff. It should be detailed enough to guide repeatable execution.

TYPICAL CONTENTS  
\- Standard\_Project\_Phases  
\- Client\_Onboarding\_and\_Kickoff  
\- Website\_Build\_Playbook  
\- Automation\_Build\_Playbook  
\- QA\_and\_Launch\_Checklist

GENERAL RULES  
1\. Make everything step-based with checklists.  
2\. Separate high-touch vs low-touch where useful.  
3\. Include “owner” where possible (who is responsible).  
4\. Keep timelines realistic and easy to adjust.
