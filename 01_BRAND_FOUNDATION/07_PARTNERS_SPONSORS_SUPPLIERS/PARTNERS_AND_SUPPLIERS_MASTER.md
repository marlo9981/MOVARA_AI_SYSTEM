# Partners and Suppliers Master

## Purpose

This file records the types of external brand relationships that can strengthen the Atrellis brand.

The goal is not to list every supplier.
The goal is to identify which relationships matter strategically and how they should be used.

## Why this matters

In renovation, design-build, and product-system categories, customers often use external cues to judge credibility.

They may trust a brand more if they see:
- credible material systems
- recognized product associations
- known supplier references
- high-quality brand ecosystem signals

These references can help Atrellis feel more established and more dependable.

## Main relationship types

### Type 1: Product-system suppliers
These are suppliers or associated brands connected to product systems that Atrellis offers or references.

Examples may include:
- paint brands
- blinds systems
- roofing systems
- material suppliers
- outdoor or structural system brands

### Type 2: Strategic partners
These are external collaborators, ecosystem relationships, or business-aligned partners that increase trust or capability.

### Type 3: Sponsors or featured brand associations
These are brands or organizations that can increase perceived legitimacy when the relationship is real and contextually relevant.

## Strategic use of these relationships

These relationships should help communicate:

- Atrellis is credible
- Atrellis works within a serious ecosystem
- Atrellis is not operating in isolation
- Atrellis understands recognized materials and systems
- Atrellis can guide customers through quality-related decisions

## Main rule

External associations should support the Atrellis story, not overpower it.

The website should still feel like Atrellis is the main trusted brand.

## Credibility rule

Every listed relationship should be:
- real
- relevant
- supportable
- useful to the customer

If a relationship is weak, ambiguous, outdated, or only loosely connected, do not use it as a major trust signal.

## Placement rule

Not every partner or supplier needs to appear on the homepage.

Use the right relationship in the right context:
- broad trust pages for major credibility signals
- service or category pages for relevant system-specific brands
- about or ecosystem pages for relationship context
