# 07_PARTNERS_SPONSORS_SUPPLIERS

## Purpose

This folder defines how Atrellis should present partners, sponsors, suppliers, and associated brands within the wider brand system.

Its role is to explain:
- which external brand relationships matter,
- how they support trust and authority,
- how they should be described,
- where they should appear,
- and how to use them without making the brand feel dependent or inflated.

This folder is important because external credibility signals can strengthen trust, but only when they are relevant, real, and placed carefully.

## What belongs here

- Partner and supplier relationship mapping
- Brand association strategy
- Placement logic for sponsor and supplier references
- Rules for using external brand names and logos
- Trust implications of ecosystem relationships

## What does not belong here

- Random logo collections
- Unverified partner claims
- Full pricing agreements
- Procurement or operational purchasing details
- Generic "we work with the best brands" statements with no structure

Those belong elsewhere.

## Usage rules

- Only include real, supportable associations.
- Use external brands to strengthen trust, not to replace Atrellis' own positioning.
- Do not overstate the depth of a relationship.
- Keep ecosystem references relevant to the service or page context.
