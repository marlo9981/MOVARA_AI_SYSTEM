\# Sales and Funnel Metrics

Purpose: define how Movara AI tracks the performance of leads and deals.

\#\# Core Funnel Stages (Example)

\- Visitor  
\- Lead (form or contact)  
\- Qualified Lead (fit \+ intent)  
\- Proposal Sent  
\- Closed Won / Closed Lost

\#\# Key Metrics

1\. Visitor → Lead Conversion Rate  
\- Definition: percentage of website visitors who submit a form or contact.  
\- Formula: leads / visitors.

2\. Lead → Qualified Lead Conversion Rate  
\- Definition: percentage of leads that fit ICP and show real interest.  
\- Formula: qualified\_leads / leads.

3\. Proposal Win Rate  
\- Definition: percentage of proposals that become closed-won deals.  
\- Formula: closed\_won / proposals\_sent.

4\. Average Deal Size  
\- Definition: average value of closed-won projects.  
\- Formula: total\_revenue\_from\_new\_deals / number\_of\_new\_deals.

5\. Sales Cycle Length  
\- Definition: average time from first meaningful contact to closed-won.  
\- Formula: average(days\_between\_first\_contact\_and\_close).
