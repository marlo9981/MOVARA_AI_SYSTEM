MOVARA AI – CORE KPIs AND DEFINITIONS

PURPOSE  
Define the key metrics Movara AI cares about so everyone uses the same language and formulas.

TOP-LEVEL AREAS  
\- Lead and funnel metrics  
\- Revenue and deal metrics  
\- Delivery and quality metrics

EXAMPLES (TO ADAPT)

Lead and Funnel  
\- Website visitors  
\- Leads captured  
\- Qualified leads (fit \+ interest)  
\- Visitor → lead conversion rate  
\- Lead → qualified lead conversion rate

Revenue and Deals  
\- Number of proposals sent  
\- Win rate (proposals → signed)  
\- Average project value  
\- Monthly and quarterly booked revenue

Delivery and Quality  
\- On-time delivery rate  
\- Number of revision cycles per project  
\- Client satisfaction (simple rating)  
\- Churn and repeat business (for recurring work)

AI WORKFLOW METRICS (NEW — April 2026)

AI Workflow ROI
- Definition: Revenue generated or costs saved directly from multi-model workflow outputs
- Formula: (Revenue from AI-assisted projects ÷ Total AI tool spend) × 100
- Target: >500x ROI
- Benchmark: $1.71 workflow cost vs. $3,000–$15,000 SGD project value

Cost Per Deliverable
- Definition: Total AI model cost to produce one client-ready output
- Formula: Sum of all model costs per project workflow
- Target: <$5.00 per deliverable
- Current benchmark: $1.71 (Research-Analysis-Workflow)

Time-to-Deliverable
- Definition: Clock time from first prompt to publish/send-ready output
- Formula: End time − Start time per workflow run
- Target: <45 minutes per deliverable
- Current benchmark: 32 min (Research-Analysis-Workflow)
